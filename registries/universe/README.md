# Universe Registry

Canonical candidate, disposition, entity, relation, provenance-link, and
deterministic universe-snapshot records belong here.

`full-known-universe-2026-08-15.v1.json` is the bounded full-universe planning
index used to forecast final corpus scale before canonical universe discovery.
It is intentionally labeled
noncanonical and does not claim discovery exhaustion or C1/C2 certification.

The later declared-cutoff exhaustion census is summarized by
[`known-universe-census-forecast.json`](../../reports/scale/known-universe-census-forecast.json)
and its [research report](../../docs/campaigns/known-universe-census-2026-08-19.md).
Those products supersede the 77-facility artifact for breadth and capacity
planning, while remaining noncanonical and preserving the detailed research
ledger outside Git.
