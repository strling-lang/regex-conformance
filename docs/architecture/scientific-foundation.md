# Scientific identity and certification foundation

The scientific foundation is the composition of four independently versioned
systems. Its acceptance is recorded by the
[foundation manifest and report](../../foundation/README.md). Passing this
integration boundary means the machinery is coherent enough for versioned
semantic reconstruction; it does not mean the incomplete data project has
passed C1-C7.

## Authority matrix

| Question | Canonical owner | Consumer rule |
| --- | --- | --- |
| What scientific entity or immutable artifact is this? | Scientific identity catalog, namespaces, constructors, and lineage | Derivation, execution, and certification reference those identities; they do not mint alternatives |
| Why is a generated assertion justified? | Generated-assertion derivation catalog | Consumers may admit or reject its evidence strength but cannot reclassify it |
| What physically happened and was it target-attributable? | Physical-attempt, terminal-observation, and logical-disposition validation | Certification consumes the validated logical disposition and does not infer behavior from process symptoms |
| Does governed evidence satisfy a certification criterion? | Versioned certification predicate evaluator and authority index | Campaign, qualification, and probe reports cannot independently issue full C1-C7 authority |

The dependency arrows do not duplicate authority. Execution uses scientific
identities and assertion derivations; certification uses all three upstream
systems. Each upstream decision remains owned and validated exactly once.

## Integrated acceptance

The acceptance evaluator proves the following as one conjunction:

1. the 22,359-identity lock and lineage rules validate;
2. the generated-assertion catalog validates without evidence-strength
   escalation;
3. the attempt/observation policy, append-only lineage, terminality, retry, and
   historical schema routes validate;
4. the versioned C1-C7 contract, current input, current evaluation, authority
   index, and fixtures validate;
5. every bound artifact in the foundation manifest has its expected digest;
6. authority ownership is unique and no legacy surface can emit an
   authoritative full certification result;
7. target-attributable completion, inconclusive retry, retry exhaustion,
   repeated measurement, target crash, and adapter crash produce the exact C5
   credit required by execution provenance;
8. identity, derivation, attempt, observation, evidence-object, and
   certification-input corruptions are detected and remain expressible as an
   independent C6 failure;
9. accepted semantic snapshot, projection, vector-requirement, and denominator
   bytes are unchanged; and
10. all tracked migration simulations preserve immutable history while
    advancing identities or revisions where meaning changes.

The generated acceptance assertions bind shared derivation revisions from the
canonical catalog. File digests and hosted results are measurements;
non-overlapping authority assignments are governance decisions; conjunctions
and fixture results are calculations. The acceptance layer does not create a
new provenance taxonomy.

## Certification boundary

The foundation acceptance report is a content-derived `trust-assessment`. It is
deliberately not a `certification-report.v1` and does not issue a C1-C7
certificate. The current scientific report remains `FAIL`: C1-C3 and C5-C7 are
`BLOCKED`, while C4 is `FAIL` at `0/9506`.

Environment, adapter, storage, Evidence Pack, and campaign qualifications may
retain truthful narrow uses of “qualified” or “certified” when their exact
scope is explicit. They cannot imply that the full data project passed C1-C7.

## Immutable history and semantic evolution

The acceptance report binds the exact source revision on which the four
foundations were jointly evaluated, their artifact digests, the semantic-input
baseline, and the previously successful hosted validation. The promoted gate
commit is recorded externally because a commit cannot contain its own hash.

Future semantic reconstruction may improve names, categories, feature facts,
sources, obligations, and generated projections only through these contracts:

- wording, category, and source-file moves retain scientific identity;
- changed meaning creates successor identity and lineage;
- splits, merges, removal, and new obligations preserve historical
  resolvability;
- generator reordering cannot churn contained scientific IDs;
- a material derivation change creates a new derivation revision; and
- a new semantic snapshot or certification contract advances current authority
  without rewriting prior reports.

Semantic work must not redefine the identity constructors, evidence-strength
classes, target-attribution rules, or certification predicates merely to make a
new corpus pass. A genuine foundation defect requires its own versioned repair
and acceptance rather than being hidden as semantic work.
