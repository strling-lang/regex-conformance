## What the gate proves

The gate freshly reads committed snapshots through the independent accounting
implementation. It does not call the primary materializer. It proves:

- all obligation and requirement stable identities are unique and attributable;
- required/conditional, scientific-purpose, execution-disposition, and
  evidence-role partitions each close on their own axis;
- the 988 additional evidence roles follow the archetype cardinality contract;
- all conditional predicates use canonical capability identities, normalize
  deterministically, and contain no tautology, contradiction, or dangling
  reference;
- every included object has a semantic assertion-to-rule-to-obligation-to-
  requirement explanation chain;
- all researched suppressions remain explicit and do not masquerade as
  prohibited or as missing profile support;
- every predecessor obligation and requirement has one migration disposition;
- the historical 12,048/9,506 artifacts retain their exact bytes; and
- no historical feature-case, profile, platform, repetition, or planning
  multiplier contributes to current authority.

The over-count attack rejects scientific questions or evidence roles whose
identity bases duplicate an existing member, operation aliases promoted as new
scope, characterization promoted to normative evidence, interaction questions
without typed attribution, and legacy template leakage. The under-count attack
requires exact accepted-question closure, at least one evidence path for every
obligation and feature, coverage of every canonical operation, a complete
feature/facet decision space, and representation of the phase, complexity, and
security facets where their researched semantics trigger obligations.

## Conditional applicability boundary

Every conditional requirement retains a normalized predicate over canonical
feature, operation, manifestation, variant, and modifier capability sets. The
bounded acceptance canary preserves three outcomes:

```text
capability known and satisfied   -> applicable
capability known and unsatisfied -> not applicable under the rule
capability fact absent           -> unresolved
```

This is an interface contract, not the final applicability evaluator. It does
not populate profile facts or infer unsupported behavior from missing data.

The lower semantic requirement scenario is 1,406. Expected remains the
symbolic expression `1,406 + sum(P(profile facts satisfy predicate r))`, with
bounds 1,406–3,378, until an accepted empirical profile model exists. The
conservative semantic ceiling is 3,378 and does not claim all conditions apply
to any one profile.

## Certification and supersession

Expensive recomputation, adversarial checks, deterministic regeneration, and
broad suites run under **Local Authoritative Certification / Hosted Integrity
Verification**. A manifest-only commit binds the exact clean source tree, gate
artifacts, catalogs, test-result root, and PASS result. Hosted Linux verifies
that envelope, recomputes cheap cross-sums and bounded canaries, and retains
veto authority without replaying the expensive audit.

The source-bound acceptance report cannot embed its own later certification
root or hosted run identifier without a circular hash or Git dependency. Those
closure facts therefore live in the certification envelope and external
governance evidence, while the report declares the required binding location.

A future semantic, derivation, denominator, or applicability successor creates
new versioned artifacts and a new acceptance. This report and its predecessor
snapshots remain immutable and resolvable.
