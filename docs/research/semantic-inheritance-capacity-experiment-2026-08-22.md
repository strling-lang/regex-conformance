# Semantic inheritance capacity experiment

Status: rejected as a production capacity strategy. This is a research record,
not a conformance rule, evidence schema, execution policy, or denominator input.

The 2026-08-22 experiment tested whether exact descendant profiles could reuse
an authoritative backend observation without losing scientific coverage. One
narrow relationship was certifiable: PCRE2 10.47 ordinary 8-bit to PCRE2 10.47
DFA 8-bit, limited to compilation and the syntax, core compile-acceptance, and
compile-error facets.

The targeted qualification performed 22 direct canary executions. Eight
compile-equivalence pairs agreed and three adversarial match/capture pairs
correctly differed. The rule would have made 2,311 coordinates theoretically
derivable, reduced the expected logical-execution forecast by only 2,289, and
saved approximately 13.944 minutes of active execution time.

After accounting for relation proofs, derivation records, canary evidence, and
integrity overhead, the conservative retained-storage forecast increased by
742,986 bytes, from 14,559,081,529 to 14,559,824,515 bytes. The production
architecture was therefore rejected and removed. No derived Supported or
Unsupported state, inheritance rule, proof schema, equivalence-class compiler,
query behavior, or capacity credit survives this experiment.

Future work should not repeat this approach as a storage optimization unless a
materially larger exact relation graph and a lower-overhead proof model are
first demonstrated independently. Shared implementation lineage alone remains
insufficient evidence for compatibility inheritance.
